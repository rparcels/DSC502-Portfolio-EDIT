Milestone 2: Fixing "Ugly" Visualizations

"Ugly" Visualization:

This caffeine rating chart is aesthetically appealing but ineffective at conveying numerical information. The chart uses cups instead of a consistent numerical scale, making accurate comparisons difficult. The horizontal spacing suggests equal intervals despite representing unequal caffeine amounts, which can mislead readers. Additionally, the use of various colors and symbols on the chart adds complexity without conveying additional information.


Improved Visualization:

This revised caffeine chart presents the data using a consistent numerical scale, allowing readers to make accurate, direct comparisons between beverages. The bars are evenly spaced and proportionally sized according to caffeine content, eliminating the ambiguity created by pictograms. The color gradient reinforces the quantitative values without adding unnecessary visual noise, resulting in a chart that is both attractive and genuinely informative.
