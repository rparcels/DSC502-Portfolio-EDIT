Milestone 1: Initial Data Exploration

Visualization 1: Scatter Plot

I chose to plot horsepower vs. 0–60 times because it represents a fundamental relationship in vehicle performance. Horsepower is often assumed to directly determine acceleration, but real‑world data shows the relationship is imperfect. This is likely due to factors such as weight, drivetrain, traction, and torque delivery. A scatter plot does a great job visualizing these patterns, and using color to distinguish Electric, Hybrid, and ICE vehicles adds clarity by showing how different powertrain types cluster and perform across the same relationship.
